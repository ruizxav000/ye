# MPC 2000XL

A 3D browser recreation of the Akai MPC 2000XL. It is built to the real proportions (about 408 × 375 × 85 mm) and floats against a "Runaway"-style dusk sky. Use the Three-quarter, Front, Top down and Back views, or drag to spin it all the way around; the rear panel has the jacks. It has a synthesized kit (808 kick and boom, snare, clap, hats, keys,
choir, brass, orchestra hit and more), live loop recording quantized to 16th notes,
note repeat, swing, four sequences, a step editor, and sample loading and chopping.

Open `index.html` in a browser. There's no build step and nothing to install.

**New to it?** Press **Lessons** above the machine. Six short lessons point at each control and wait for you to use it: the pads, playing loops, recording a beat, editing on the screen, note repeat and 16 Levels, and chopping a sample.

| 1–4 | `Z` `X` `C` `V` |

## Controls

- **Screen:** click a value or move to it with the cursor keys (`←` `→`). Change it with the data wheel, `↑` `↓`, or the keypad followed by Enter.
- **F1–F6:** bars, copy sequence, clear sequence, metronome, load or unload a sample, chop a sample across pads 9–16.
- **Rec / Over dub / Stop / Play / Play start.** Press Stop twice to go back to the start.
- **Tap tempo / note repeat:** tap for tempo; hold it while playing pads to roll them.
- **Erase:** hold it and tap a pad to wipe that pad's hits.
- **Full level / 16 levels:** turn Full level off to make hits louder toward the top of a pad. 16 levels plays the selected sound chromatically across all pads, with pad 5 at its original pitch.
- **Next seq / Track mute,** plus Filter and Main volume knobs.

Keyboard: `Space` play from start/stop · `Enter` record · `T` tap tempo · `N` latch note repeat · `M` metronome · `[` `]` tempo · `Ctrl/Cmd+Z` undo
