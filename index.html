Drums

Drop an audio file on any pad to sample it. Samples stay loaded until you reload; patterns and settings are remembered in this browser.

Step edit · Seq A

Click or drag across cells to place hits. Faded cells are softer hits; numbers are 16 Levels tunings. M mutes a track.

Record a loop

Press Over dub to start recording right away, or arm Rec and press Play start. Hits snap to the nearest sixteenth and loop back each pass. Undo seq takes back the last pass.

Edit on the screen

Click a value on the LCD or move to it with the cursor keys. Turn the data wheel, press ▲ ▼, or type a number on the keypad and press Enter.

16 Levels

Select a pad and turn on 16 Levels. All sixteen pads now play that sound in semitones, with pad 5 at its original pitch. Chop a soul record with F6, then play a slice up high for the sped-up vocal sound.

Keyboard

Pads are 1–4 Q–R A–F Z–V, top row to bottom. Space plays from the top and stops, Enter arms record, T taps tempo, N latches note repeat, arrow keys move around the screen.

Repeat, erase, mute

Hold Tap tempo while playing pads to roll them. Hold Erase and tap a pad to wipe its hits. Turn on Track mute and tap pads to drop parts in and out.
