Sequence A

Click or drag across cells to place hits. M mutes a track. Sequences A and B start with example loops; C and D are blank.

Recording a loop

Press Rec, then Play. Every pad you hit lands on the nearest sixteenth note and loops back on the next pass. Play another layer on top the same way. Undo takes back the last pass.

Chopping a sample

Chop to 9–16 slices an audio file into eight equal pieces across the top two rows, the way soul records get flipped. Retune each slice with the Tune fader.

Swing

Swing pushes every second sixteenth late. 50% is straight; around 54–58% gives the loose head-nod feel the classic samplers are known for; 66% is a full triplet shuffle.
